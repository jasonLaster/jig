# Natural Walnut Veneer

Author: Jenelle van Heerden / Poly Haven
Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/natural_walnut_veneer
License: CC0 1.0 (https://polyhaven.com/license)
Retrieved: 2026-09-16

Base color, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps downloaded at 2K and converted
from JPEG to WebP at quality 90. Grain runs horizontally. Source texture covers
approximately 1 m x 1 m. These are walnut veneer appearance references, not a
Bastogne or Claro species identification or a veneer construction specification.
